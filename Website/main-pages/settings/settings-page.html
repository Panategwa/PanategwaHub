What is synced, and what is only on this device Every setting on this page is stored in this browser only. None of them are uploaded, which is worth knowing before you reset or clear them.
Only on this device
Theme, text size, language, animation level, sidebar width, the three audio levels, which music track was playing and which tracks you have switched off. They live in this browser's local storage, so they follow you between pages and survive closing the tab, but they do not follow you to another device or another browser. Clearing site data here removes them and only here.
Kept on your account
Your sign-in details, profile, friends and messages, achievements and the site time you have spent. Those are the things that follow you between devices, and none of them are touched by the Reset button below.
Reset all settings Puts every setting on this page back to how the site ships: default theme, default text size, English, Normal animations, the default sidebar width and the default audio levels. Your account, your progress and your achievements are not touched.
